# Product: AuditFlow

- **Users.** Audit staff at a Malaysian chartered accountancy firm: associates, seniors, managers and partners. They use it at office desks in daylight for long spreadsheet-heavy sessions.
- **Job.** Turn a client's balance sheet and P&L into a complete, referenced, reviewable audit file in the firm's format, without arithmetic errors, with a record of who did what.
- **Tone.** A quiet, precise professional tool. It should feel like a well-kept working paper, not a marketing product.
- **Musts.**
  - Every upload is signed by its uploader, and unsigned files are never processed.
  - Tracking is visible to staff.
  - AI output is always a draft for a named person.
  - Numbers are shown in accounting format with brackets for credits.
- **Assumptions.** Written from the brief without a user interview, because the session was autonomous.
