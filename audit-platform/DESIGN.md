# Design: working-paper world

- **Mode.** Operate. Light theme chosen for daytime office use alongside spreadsheets and printed papers. The dark theme suggested by UI UX Pro Max was rejected for this use.
- **Colour.**
  - Warm paper `#faf8f3`, white surfaces, second neutral `#f2efe7` for the sidebar.
  - Ink text `#1c2321`, with secondary text `#4a5451` and `#69726f`.
  - One accent, ledger green `#0f5e4a`, for primary actions, selection and "done" states.
  - Semantic colours for state only: warning `#8a5300`, danger `#a12a1f`, info `#1f4e79`.
- **Type.** IBM Plex Sans throughout, self-hosted. Figures use tabular numerals. IBM Plex Mono only for working-paper references and hashes. Scale: 12, 13, 14 (body), 16, 20 and 24 px.
- **Spacing.** 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48 and 64 px.
- **Tables.** Ledger style: sticky shelf-coloured header, hairline rules, subtotal rows with a top rule, grand totals with a double underline.
- **Motion.** 150 ms colour transitions only. Reduced-motion is respected.
- **Components.** Buttons in four variants (primary, secondary, ghost, danger), badges, notices, a sectioned page layout with hairline dividers instead of cards.
